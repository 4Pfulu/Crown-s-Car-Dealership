## Project Overview
Crown's Car Dealership is a fictional small, independently owned car dealership based in Pretoria, Gauteng, specialising in new and pre-owned vehicles. This project is part of a three-part Portfolio of Evidence for WEDE5020, in which a fully functional, responsive, and SEO-optimised website is developed for the dealership. Part 1 focuses on planning, research, and building the foundational HTML structure of the site.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Establish a professional online presence for Crown's Car Dealership.
- Allow potential customers to browse available vehicle stock online.
- Generate qualified sales leads through an online enquiry form.
- Provide clear, accessible information on services, financing, and contact details.

## Key Features and Functionality
- Responsive navigation menu linking all pages.
- Homepage with hero section and featured vehicles.
- Vehicle listings page (Products).
- Enquiry form for product and finance enquiries.
- Contact page with two branch locations and a general contact form.

## Timeline and Milestones
- **Week 1–2:** Research, content gathering, sitemap, and wireframes.
- **Week 3–4:** HTML structure and navigation for all five pages.
- **Week 5:** Content integration, testing, and GitHub commits.
- **Week 6:** Final review, debugging, and submission.

## Part 1 Details
Part 1 covers project planning, content research, and the foundational HTML structure of the website, including semantic HTML, navigation, and basic content integration. Part 2 (CSS styling) and Part 3 (JavaScript functionality) will follow in future submissions.
